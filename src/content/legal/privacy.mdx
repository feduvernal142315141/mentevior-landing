MenteVior LLC (“MenteVior,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides an electronic health record, practice management, billing, communications, analytics, and artificial intelligence-assisted platform for healthcare providers in the United States (the “Services”). We also operate the website at mentevior.com (the “Website”).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information through the Website and Services. It also explains the choices and rights that may be available to individuals.

## Scope and Our Role

This Privacy Policy applies to:

- visitors to the Website;
- representatives of prospective and current customers;
- healthcare providers and other authorized workforce members who use the Services;
- individuals who communicate with us or request support; and
- patients, clients, caregivers, or other individuals who access a feature of the Services at the invitation or direction of a healthcare provider customer.

MenteVior’s healthcare provider customers determine what patient or client information is entered into the Services and how that information is used. When MenteVior creates, receives, maintains, or transmits protected health information (“PHI”) for a customer that is a covered entity or business associate under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 and its implementing regulations (“HIPAA”), MenteVior acts as that customer’s business associate. Our handling of PHI is governed by HIPAA and the applicable business associate agreement (“BAA”). The BAA controls if it conflicts with this Privacy Policy with respect to PHI.

<LegalCallout title="This is not your provider’s Notice of Privacy Practices">
This Privacy Policy is not a healthcare provider’s Notice of Privacy Practices. Patients and clients should review their provider’s Notice of Privacy Practices and contact the provider directly with questions or requests concerning their medical or clinical records. Under HIPAA, covered healthcare providers are generally responsible for responding to individual requests for access, amendment, and an accounting of disclosures, while their business associates assist as required (U.S. Department of Health and Human Services).
</LegalCallout>

## Information We Collect

Depending on how a person interacts with MenteVior, we may collect the following categories of information.

### Information Provided Directly to Us

- **Contact and professional information:** name, business email address, telephone number, organization, job title, professional role, specialty, and information submitted through forms or demo requests.
- **Account information:** username, password or authentication credentials, customer organization, account status, permissions, and user preferences.
- **Communications:** messages, support requests, feedback, survey responses, and other correspondence with MenteVior.
- **Billing and transaction information:** billing contact, subscription, invoice, transaction, and limited payment-related information. Payment card details are collected and processed by our payment processor. MenteVior does not store full payment card numbers.

### Customer Data and PHI

Customers and their authorized users may submit information to the Services, including:

- patient or client names, contact details, dates of birth, demographic information, and identifiers;
- insurance, eligibility, authorization, billing, claims, and payment information;
- diagnoses, assessments, treatment plans, clinical notes, progress data, medications, provider information, appointment information, uploaded documents, and other health or behavioral health information;
- communications among providers, staff, patients, clients, caregivers, and other authorized participants; and
- information concerning minors or other dependents when submitted by an authorized healthcare provider or user.

We refer to this information collectively as “Customer Data.” Customer Data may include PHI and other sensitive personal information. The customer controls Customer Data, and MenteVior processes it only to provide the Services, as directed by the customer, as permitted by the applicable agreement and BAA, or as required by law.

### AI Feature Information

When an authorized user uses an AI-assisted feature, we may process the prompts, instructions, files, clinical information, and other content submitted to the feature, along with its output. PHI may be processed only through AI providers that are contractually authorized to handle it and that have entered into a BAA where required. **MenteVior and its AI providers do not use Customer Data or PHI to train general-purpose AI models.**

AI-generated output may be incomplete or inaccurate and does not replace professional judgment. Authorized users are responsible for reviewing and validating output before relying on it for clinical, billing, operational, or other purposes.

### Information Collected Automatically

When a person uses the Website or Services, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

- internet protocol address, device identifiers, browser type, operating system, and device information;
- dates and times of access, pages or features viewed, links selected, session activity, referring pages, and general usage information;
- approximate location inferred from an internet protocol address; and
- diagnostic, security, error, performance, and audit-log information.

We use cookies, local storage, software development kits, and similar technologies for authentication, security, preferences, functionality, and basic website or product analytics. **We do not use this information for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising.**

## Sources of Information

We collect personal information:

- directly from individuals;
- from healthcare provider customers and their authorized users;
- automatically from browsers, devices, and use of the Website or Services;
- from payment processors and other service providers;
- from referral sources, business partners, or publicly available professional sources; and
- as directed or authorized by a customer.

## How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

- provide, operate, maintain, support, and improve the Website and Services;
- create and administer customer and user accounts;
- process subscriptions, invoices, and payments;
- provide clinical documentation, practice management, billing, messaging, analytics, and AI-assisted features;
- authenticate users, apply permissions, maintain audit logs, prevent fraud, investigate misuse, and protect the security and integrity of the Services;
- respond to inquiries, support requests, feedback, and complaints;
- communicate about accounts, transactions, security, service changes, and administrative matters;
- send product, service, or marketing communications where permitted by law and honor opt-out requests;
- analyze Website and product performance and understand feature usage;
- comply with legal obligations, BAAs, contracts, court orders, and lawful government requests;
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims;
- enforce our agreements and policies; and
- support a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, subject to applicable law and contractual restrictions.

We do not use PHI for MenteVior’s independent marketing. We use and disclose PHI only as permitted by the applicable BAA, customer instructions, and law.

## How We Disclose Information

We may disclose personal information to the following categories of recipients:

- **Healthcare provider customers:** to the customer that controls the applicable account or Customer Data and to its authorized users.
- **Service providers and subcontractors:** to vendors that support hosting, storage, security, authentication, analytics, AI functionality, payment processing, communications, customer support, and other business operations. Service providers may use information only to perform services for MenteVior or as otherwise permitted by contract and law. A subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on our behalf must be bound by a BAA where required.
- **At a customer’s or user’s direction:** to persons or organizations selected or authorized by the customer or user, including through integrations, exports, referrals, communications, or other enabled workflows.
- **Professional advisors:** to attorneys, auditors, insurers, accountants, and consultants that require access to perform professional services and are subject to appropriate confidentiality duties.
- **Government authorities and legal recipients:** when we reasonably believe disclosure is required by law, subpoena, court order, or lawful government request; necessary to investigate unlawful conduct or enforce our agreements; or necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of MenteVior, our customers, users, or others.
- **Business transaction participants:** in connection with a proposed or completed financing, merger, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, sale of assets, or similar transaction. PHI remains subject to applicable law and BAAs.

**MenteVior does not sell personal information for money.** MenteVior also does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising and does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

## HIPAA and Patient or Client Requests

HIPAA requires covered entities and business associates to use written BAAs that limit permitted uses and disclosures of PHI and require appropriate safeguards (U.S. Department of Health and Human Services). MenteVior uses and discloses PHI in accordance with its BAAs and supports customers in meeting applicable obligations.

If you are a patient, client, caregiver, or personal representative seeking to access, correct, amend, restrict, delete, or obtain an accounting concerning clinical or billing information held in the Services, contact the healthcare provider that collected the information. MenteVior will assist the provider as required by the applicable BAA and law. We may refer a request concerning Customer Data to the relevant provider.

## Data Retention and Account Termination

We retain Website information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law, including for dispute resolution, legal holds, security, fraud prevention, or compliance.

After a customer’s subscription ends, MenteVior makes the customer’s data available for export for sixty days. During that period, the customer may instruct MenteVior to return or destroy the data, subject to the applicable agreement, BAA, and law. After the sixty-day export period, MenteVior has no obligation to retain the data and may delete it at any time. MenteVior does not offer extended or archival retention. Customers are responsible for exporting and independently retaining records they are required to keep.

We retain information MenteVior holds in its own right, such as marketing Website and account records, for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected and to meet legal obligations.

## Security

MenteVior maintains reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. These safeguards include encryption in transit and at rest, unique user accounts and role-based access controls, and security and activity audit logging. Our safeguards are designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic PHI consistent with applicable requirements of the HIPAA Security Rule (U.S. Department of Health and Human Services).

Customers are responsible for managing authorized users, assigning appropriate permissions, protecting credentials, securing devices and networks, and promptly notifying MenteVior of suspected unauthorized access. No method of internet transmission, electronic storage, or security control is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Privacy Choices

- **Account information:** authorized users may review or update certain account information through the Services or by contacting their organization’s administrator.
- **Marketing communications:** recipients may unsubscribe using the link in a marketing email or by contacting us. We may still send transactional, security, support, or other non-promotional messages.
- **Cookies and analytics:** browser settings may allow users to block or delete cookies. Blocking necessary technologies may prevent parts of the Website or Services from functioning.
- **Customer Data:** requests concerning clinical, patient, client, or billing information should be directed to the healthcare provider that controls the information.

Because MenteVior does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising and does not use it for targeted advertising, we do not offer an opt-out for those practices. Where required by applicable law, we recognize valid browser-based opt-out preference signals for processing to which the signal applies.

## U.S. State Privacy Rights

Depending on a person’s state of residence and subject to legal exceptions, applicable law may provide rights to:

- confirm whether we process personal information;
- access or obtain a copy of personal information;
- correct inaccurate personal information;
- delete personal information;
- obtain personal information in a portable format;
- opt out of a sale, targeted advertising, or certain profiling;
- limit certain uses or disclosures of sensitive personal information;
- appeal our decision concerning a request; and
- receive equal service and not be discriminated against for exercising a privacy right.

For example, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, provides qualifying California residents with rights to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, and receive non-discriminatory treatment (California Office of the Attorney General).

To submit a request concerning information MenteVior controls, email [info@mentevior.com](mailto:info@mentevior.com?subject=Privacy%20Request) with the subject line “Privacy Request.” Please state your name, state of residence, relationship with MenteVior, and the right you wish to exercise. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity and authority. An authorized agent may submit a request where permitted by law, but we may require proof of authorization and verification of the individual’s identity. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

If we deny a request and applicable law provides a right to appeal, reply to our decision or email [info@mentevior.com](mailto:info@mentevior.com?subject=Privacy%20Appeal) with the subject line “Privacy Appeal.” We will not discriminate against an individual for exercising an applicable privacy right.

These rights do not apply in all circumstances. Certain information may be exempt, including PHI governed by HIPAA, information processed solely on behalf of a customer, and information that we must retain to comply with law or protect legal rights. Requests concerning Customer Data should be submitted to the healthcare provider that controls that data.

## Children and Minors

The Website and user accounts are intended for healthcare providers and other authorized adults and are not directed to children under 13. MenteVior does not knowingly invite children under 13 to create their own general Website or provider accounts.

A healthcare provider may use the Services to maintain records about a minor or may authorize a minor’s parent, guardian, or other representative to use an appropriate Service feature. In those circumstances, MenteVior processes the information on behalf of and at the direction of the healthcare provider, subject to the applicable agreement, BAA, and law. Questions or requests concerning a minor’s clinical record should be directed to the provider.

## Third-Party Services and Integrations

The Website or Services may contain links to third-party websites or allow a customer to enable an integration with a third-party service. This Privacy Policy does not govern a third party’s independent privacy practices. Customers and users should review the third party’s privacy policy before enabling an integration or providing information directly to that party.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Services, our practices, or applicable law. We will post the updated policy and revise the “Last Updated” date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before a material change takes effect.
